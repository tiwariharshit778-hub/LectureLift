About Lecture lift....

LectureLift is an AI-powered learning platform that turns recorded lectures into personalised study material.

The idea came from a simple problem: whenever we attend a lecture, we may have the recording, but going through a long recording again and again just to revise the important parts can be time-consuming.

LectureLift allows users to upload their lecture recording, and AI processes the lecture and turns it into structured learning material such as notes, flashcards, quizzes, and mind maps.

What makes LectureLift different is that the content is created from the actual lecture, so users can learn and revise based on what was actually taught rather than starting from generic information.

The vision is to make recorded learning more useful — turning passive lecture recordings into an active, personalised learning experience.
